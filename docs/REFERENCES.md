# References

Q1 Robot SDK is an **original Quanta Computer (廣達電腦) · MIT Taiwan** education platform.

Public robotics literature on DDS, ROS 2, and RL Sim2Sim/Sim2Real informed the curriculum structure, but Q1’s mechanical design, QDD actuation stack, 3-finger hand, and transformable wheel-foot morphology are native Quanta MIT designs — not derivatives of other commercial humanoids.
